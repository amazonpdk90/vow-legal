Delete your Vow: Habits & Books account

Vow: Habits & Books  ·  by SentinelPanda LLC  ·  Effective: 22 November 2026

Option 1: delete it in the app

  1. Open Vow and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Scroll to Danger zone and tap Delete Account.
  3. Confirm with Delete Forever.

This takes effect immediately. If you only used Vow without signing in (as a guest), there is no server account: the same button wipes the app's data from your device.

Option 2: request deletion by email

No longer have the app? Email support@sentinelpanda.com from the address you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my Vow account". If you signed in with Apple using a private relay address, tell us that and we will confirm your identity another way. We delete the account within 30 days and reply to confirm. You can also use this route to ask us to remove copies of support messages or sign-in emails held in our email inboxes.

What is deleted

  • Your account: email address, name and sign-in details.
  • Your cloud backup: habits, habit history, notes, journal entries and mood entries.
  • The text of support messages you sent while signed in (erased from our database).
  • When you delete from the app, all Vow data on that device as well: habits, notes, journal, mood, photos, downloaded and imported books, and files you exported with Vow.
  • When we handle a deletion request by email, copies of your support messages in our support inbox, and sign-in emails in the sending mailbox.

What may be kept, and for how long

  • Server backups: up to 14 days. We keep a nightly encrypted copy of our database on a server we operate and, if enabled, an encrypted off-site copy. Both are deleted after 14 days, so your data disappears from backups within 14 days. If a backup ever has to be restored, deleted accounts are removed again first.
  • A record that a support request of a given category was filed, with its text erased and no link to you.
  • Emailed copies of support messages, if you deleted in the app and did not also email us: up to 12 months, then deleted (email us to have them removed sooner).
  • Purchase records held by Google Play and RevenueCat, as required for billing and tax, for as long as needed for those purposes. They are kept under a random or account ID, without your name or email, and are not deleted by us. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription: cancel it in Google Play → Subscriptions.
  • Crash reports are kept for 30 days and then expire.

If you used Vow without signing in, your data only existed on your device (apart from the automatic connections described in the Privacy Policy): uninstalling the app, or Profile → Reset All Data, removes it.
