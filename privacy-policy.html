Privacy Policy

Vow: Habits & Books  ·  Effective: 22 November 2026

Vow: Habits & Books ("Vow", "we", "us", "the app") is operated by SentinelPanda LLC, a company registered in the United States, which is the data controller. Contact: support@sentinelpanda.com. Postal address: available on request by email. Your habits, notes, journal, mood entries and photos live only on your device unless you turn on an optional feature that sends them off it. A few technical connections happen automatically; they are all listed below. This policy explains what is collected, when, why, and what you can do about it.

1. Quick summary

  • The app works offline. Even if you never sign in, it contacts our subscription provider RevenueCat when it starts (a random ID plus standard technical details such as IP address and device and app version, so we can tell whether Pro is unlocked) and it sends crash reports to Sentry unless you turn them off. Your habits, notes, journal, mood entries and photos stay on your device unless you turn on Cloud Backup or send us a support message.
  • Searching for or downloading free books contacts Project Gutenberg (gutenberg.org, or its own mirror gutenberg.pglaf.org when the main site is down).
  • Optional Cloud Backup (free with an account) copies habits, habit history, notes, journal and mood entries to our server in the USA. It is off by default and the app asks you before turning it on.
  • Crash reports contain technical details only; we do not intentionally include what you type. You can switch them off in the app.
  • We never sell or rent personal data, we show no ads, and we never use your data to train AI models. We do not currently send your data to any AI service. If that ever changes, we will update this policy and ask for your consent first.
  • You can export your data, or delete your account and its cloud data, from the app.

2. Data stored on your device

  • Habits & history: names, descriptions, categories, schedules, reminders, completion status, streaks.
  • Notes: title, body, tags.
  • Journal: gratitude text, daily action, reflections, 30-day commitment.
  • Mood log: a 1–5 rating per day.
  • Photo proofs: stored on the device. Taking a photo uses the camera permission, which is asked only when you choose to take one. Choosing an existing photo uses Android's system photo picker, so Vow never gets access to your photo library. Photos are never uploaded, not even with Cloud Backup on.
  • Books: files you import or download, reading progress, highlights and bookmarks.
  • App preferences: theme, language, accent colour, notification times.

This data stays on the device unless you use one of the features in sections 3 to 6. Vow does not ask for location, contacts, microphone or storage permissions.

3. Account data (only if you sign in)

You can sign in with Google or with your email: we send a 6-digit code to your address, which you type into the app. (Apple sign-in exists only in iOS builds, which are not currently released.) Signing in creates an account on Supabase (see section 8). It holds your email address, your name if the provider shares it, the sign-in provider, and a unique user ID. Sign-in code emails are sent by Supabase Auth through Google's Gmail email service. A copy of your name, email and user ID is also kept on the device in encrypted secure storage.

4. Cloud Backup (only if you turn it on)

Before Cloud Backup is turned on, the app asks you to confirm that your habits, notes, journal and mood entries will be uploaded to a server in the USA. Once on, the following is stored on Supabase (AWS us-east-1, Virginia, USA) and linked to your account:

  • Habits, habit history (completion logs), notes, journal entries, mood entries.
  • Timestamps of your last changes.

Photos and book files are never backed up. If you sign in on a new device and that device has no habits yet, the app restores your backup automatically.

Mood and journal entries can reveal information about your health or wellbeing. By turning Cloud Backup on you explicitly consent to us storing them. You can withdraw that consent at any time by turning Cloud Backup off (nothing new is uploaded) or by deleting your account (all cloud data is deleted). Deleting a habit or a note in the app also removes its cloud copy, and journal and mood copies are overwritten at each backup while Cloud Backup is on.

5. Support requests (only if you contact us)

Messages sent from the in-app Support form are stored on Supabase and forwarded by email through Resend (USA) to the developer's support inbox. Each message includes what you write, the category, and technical details: platform, OS version, app version, whether you have Pro, and your user ID if you are signed in. A message sent without an account cannot be linked to you by the app. If the form can't reach the server, the app opens your own email app instead, and your email provider and address are then involved. Please don't put sensitive information in a support message.

6. Subscriptions, crash reports and books

  • Subscriptions. Purchases are processed by Google Play, never by us. RevenueCat (USA) is contacted when the app starts, for every user including guests, and validates purchases. It receives a random ID (or your account ID if you signed in), the purchase token, your IP address, and device and app details. It does not receive your name or email.
  • Crash reports. If the app crashes, Sentry (USA) receives the error details and technical information such as device model, OS version and app version. We do not intentionally include your habit, note or journal text, we strip email addresses, and we remove query strings from web addresses before sending, but an error message can occasionally contain a fragment of text you entered. We do not send performance traces. Crash reports are on by default; turn them off in Profile → Data & support → Crash reports.
  • Books. Searching and downloading public-domain books contacts Project Gutenberg (gutenberg.org, USA). It receives your search terms and the books you download, along with your IP address as with any website. No account data is sent. Project Gutenberg is an independent third party and is not affiliated with Vow.

9. Data security

  • Sign-in tokens are stored with expo-secure-store (Android Keystore).
  • Local data is stored in an on-device SQLite database and is excluded from Android device backups.
  • All network traffic uses HTTPS/TLS; cleartext traffic is blocked by Android's network security config.
  • Cloud data is protected by Supabase Row-Level Security: each account can only read or write its own rows, enforced on the server.
  • Data is encrypted in transit and at rest (Supabase default). Our server backups are encrypted.
  • API keys for email services are held on the server, never in the app.
  • Permissions: camera (only when you take a proof photo), notifications, vibration, restoring reminders after a restart, and internet access. There is no storage, photo-library, location, contacts or microphone permission.

10. Your rights and controls

Under the GDPR and UK GDPR you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict and object to the processing of your data, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. In the app and by email:

  • Access / portability — Profile → Export Data (JSON; CSV and Markdown with Pro).
  • Rectification — edit your data in the app, or email us for account details.
  • Erase this device — Profile → Reset All Data removes habits, history, notes, journal, mood, photos and settings from the device. It does not touch your cloud backup.
  • Stop backing up / withdraw consent — turn Cloud Backup off in Profile. Nothing new is uploaded; data already backed up stays until you delete it or your account.
  • Delete everything — Profile → Danger zone → Delete Account permanently deletes your account and all cloud data (habits, history, notes, journal, mood), erases the text of support messages you sent while signed in from our database, and removes all app data from the device, including files you exported with Vow.
  • Object / restrict — turn crash reports off in Profile → Data & support → Crash reports, or email us.
  • No longer have the app? See how to request account deletion by email.
  • For anything else, including erasing a support message sent without an account, or copies of support or sign-in emails, email us (section 14). We respond within 30 days.

You can complain to your local data-protection authority (in the EU or UK, the authority in your country). We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are used in US state privacy laws.

11. Children and age

Vow is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are under the age of digital consent in your country (13 to 16, depending on the country), please ask a parent before turning on Cloud Backup. If you believe a child under 13 has used the app, contact us and we will delete the account.

12. International transfers

The services in section 8 are based in the United States, and SentinelPanda LLC is a United States company. If you are outside the US and use a feature that sends data off the device, that data is transferred to and processed in the US. For EU and UK users we rely on standard contractual clauses or an equivalent safeguard where the GDPR or UK GDPR requires one.

13. Retention

  • Device data: until you delete it, use Reset All Data, delete your account, or uninstall the app.
  • Account and cloud backup: while your account exists. Deleted when you delete your account.
  • Support messages: 12 months from receipt, after which their text is erased from our database; erased earlier if you delete your account. Copies emailed to our support inbox are kept for up to 12 months and deleted earlier when we handle a deletion request.
  • Server backups: we keep a nightly encrypted copy of our database (including account emails and backed-up content) on a server we operate and, if enabled, an encrypted copy in an off-site storage bucket. Both are deleted after 14 days, so deleted accounts disappear from backups within 14 days. If a backup ever has to be restored, deleted accounts are removed again first.
  • Sign-in code emails: the email provider's sent-mail copies may remain until we delete them when handling a deletion request.
  • Sentry crash data: 30 days.
  • Purchase records: held by Google Play and RevenueCat for as long as needed for billing, support and legal obligations, under a random or account ID, without your name or email. Deleting your account does not delete them or cancel a subscription.
  • Project Gutenberg and GitHub keep their own logs under their own policies.
